Where did Doliv lose share last quarter, and to whom?
{{ t.l }}

Doliv lost 2.1 points of category share between April and July. Three quarters of that loss sits in Abidjan, and most of it moved to one competitor.

Cocody and Yopougon account for 1.6 of the 2.1 points. Paraxel took 1.3 of those, entirely in the 1 g sachet format, which entered 214 of the 612 panel pharmacies over the same window. Outside Abidjan the share is flat.
